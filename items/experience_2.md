# Experience 2: Starting My 3D Printing Business

During university, I started my own small 3D printing business called 3DJellyPrints because I was interested in designing websites and wanted to apply my design skills outside of school. I created a website for my business, offered custom 3D printing services, communicated with customers, and managed orders. I enjoy combining my interests in design, technology, and business. One of my favourite memories was seeing my ideas turn into a real business with its own website and services that customers could use. It made me feel proud knowing that I had created something on my own. However, one thing I didn't enjoy was managing multiple responsibilities at the same time, especially when I had school assignments and customer orders to complete. It was sometimes stressful to manage my time and meet deadlines. Overall, I would give this experience an A because it taught me important skills in communication, website design, time management, and problem-solving. It also allowed me to apply what I learned in UX Design to a real business.
