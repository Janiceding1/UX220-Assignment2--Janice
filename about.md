# About Me

Hi! My name is Janice Ding, and I am a third-year User Experience Design student at Wilfrid Laurier University. I am interested in making web design more accessible for users. My future career goal is to become a UX designer at a financial institution, specifically TD Bank, where I hope to improve digital banking experiences for customers of different ages and backgrounds.

Outside of school, I enjoy playing racquet sports, including badminton, table tennis, tennis, and pickleball. I also enjoy travelling, exploring different cultures, and learning new things. During university, I started my own small 3D printing business called 3DJellyPrints, where I have been able to apply my interests in website design, technology, and business.
