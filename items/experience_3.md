# Experience 3: Studying User Experience Design at Wilfrid Laurier University

I started studying User Experience Design at Wilfrid Laurier University in 2024 because I was interested in learning how websites and applications are designed to improve people's experiences. Throughout my university studies, I have learned about UX research, design principles, accessibility, prototyping, and web development. One thing I enjoy about studying UX Design is working on UX projects where I could identify a problem, research possible solutions, and turn my ideas into designs or prototypes. I also enjoyed learning how small design decisions can make a big difference in how people use a product. However, one thing I do not enjoy is managing multiple assignments and deadlines while commuting between Waterloo and Brantford for classes. The long commute can be tiring and sometimes makes it difficult to balance school and personal responsibilities. Overall, I would give this experience a B because although university can be challenging and stressful, I have enjoyed learning new skills and working on projects that relate to my future career goal of becoming a UX designer at TD Bank.
