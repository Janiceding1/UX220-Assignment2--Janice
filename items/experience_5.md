I started playing badminton when I was younger and continued playing throughout high school and university. During university, I regularly played badminton with friends and family during my free time, especially on weekends. One thing I enjoyed about badminton was being able to stay active while spending time with people I care about. It also gave me an opportunity to take a break from schoolwork and reduce stress after a busy week. One of my favourite memories was playing badminton with friends and family at recreational centres because we could have fun, challenge each other, and improve our skills together. I also enjoyed seeing myself improve after practising different techniques and playing against stronger opponents. However, one thing I didn't enjoy was how difficult it could be to find time to play regularly, especially when I had multiple assignments, classes, and other responsibilities. Sometimes it was also frustrating when I wasn't performing as well as I wanted to. Overall, I would give this experience an A because badminton has helped me maintain a healthy lifestyle, improve my skills, and create good memories with friends and family. It also connects to my future goal of maintaining a good work-life balance while working full-time as a UX designer.
