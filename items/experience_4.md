# Experience 4: Playing Competitive Table Tennis

I started playing table tennis when I was younger and continued practising and competing throughout high school. In 2023, I participated in the Peel regional girls' table tennis competition and placed second. One thing I enjoyed about playing table tennis was improving my skills through regular practice. One of my favourite memories was placing second in the regional competition because it made me feel proud of the time and effort I had spent practising. It was rewarding to see my hard work lead to an achievement. However, one thing I didn't enjoy was the pressure of competing, especially when playing against stronger opponents or making mistakes during important matches. Sometimes losing a match could be frustrating, but it taught me how to learn from my mistakes and continue improving. Overall, I would give this experience an A because table tennis taught me how to be competitive when needed and helped me stay active.
