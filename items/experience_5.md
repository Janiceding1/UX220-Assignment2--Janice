Experience 5: Playing Badminton

I started playing badminton when I was younger and continued playing throughout high school and university. During university, I joined a badminton club where I could regularly play with other students who shared the same interest in the sport. One thing I enjoyed about being part of the club was having the opportunity to meet new people, stay active, and improve my badminton skills while taking a break from schoolwork. It also helped me reduce stress after a busy week of classes and assignments. One of my favourite memories was playing friendly matches with other club members because we could challenge each other, learn different techniques, and improve our skills together. I also enjoyed meeting students from different programs and making new friends through a shared interest. However, one thing I didn't enjoy was how difficult it could be to attend club activities regularly, especially when I had multiple assignments, classes, and other responsibilities. Sometimes it was also frustrating when I wasn't performing as well as I wanted to during matches. Overall, I would give this experience an A because joining the university badminton club helped me maintain a healthy lifestyle, improve my skills, and build friendships with other students. It also connects to my future goal of maintaining a good work-life balance while working full-time as a UX designer.
